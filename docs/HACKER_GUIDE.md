## Submission & Judging Deliverables

Submissions are due at **11:30 AM on Sunday, October 25**. Three core deliverables are evaluated:

1. **Live Presentation / Demo**:
   - Maximum 8 minutes.
   - Delivered to challenge sponsor judges and DuQuantum organizers.
   - Structure should focus on problem understanding, theoretical formulation, ownership of the design choices, reproducible numerics, and challenges solved.
2. **Project Repository**:
   - Complete codebase pushed to GitHub and linked on Devpost.
3. **Video Demo**:
   - Maximum 4 minutes, submitted to Devpost.

### Evaluation Criteria
- **Deep Understanding**: Theoretical clarity and problem formulation.
- **Ownership**: Demonstrating personal understanding and reasoning behind algorithmic choices.
- **Reproducible Numerics**: Validating state preparation on simulators and under realistic noise.
- **Sponsor Alignment**: Meeting all constraints defined in the challenge prompt.
